(0:45 demo) Every finding gets a NIST IR 8547 status, a 0–100 score and plain-language reasons. The key idea is Mosca's inequality: if the years the data must stay secret plus the years migration takes exceed the years until a quantum computer exists, the data is already at risk. hr-records holds 25-year data, so it is urgent even though its TLS 1.3 looks modern. Move the years-to-quantum slider in the demo and watch the urgent flags change. Readiness is the share of assets at OK or low severity: 30.4% before migration. Broken algorithms like MD5 and RC4 score 100 regardless of quantum.
