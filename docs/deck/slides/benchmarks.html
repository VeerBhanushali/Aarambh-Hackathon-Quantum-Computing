(0:30) [Before presenting: re-run benchmarks on the presentation laptop and replace these numbers and the machine line in the footer; the dashboard's Key takeaways box gives the sentences.] The honest story: ML-KEM is faster than RSA and even X25519 on this machine, so post-quantum key exchange costs almost nothing in CPU. Signatures are where the cost sits: ML-DSA signing is tens of times slower than ECDSA and signatures are about 46 times larger. In a real handshake hybrid key exchange adds about 2.3 KB, while a full ML-DSA certificate chain pushes the handshake past 13 KB. Handshake wall time is dominated by process start-up, so we compare bytes, not milliseconds.
