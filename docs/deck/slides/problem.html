(0:30) An attacker can record encrypted traffic today and decrypt it once a cryptographically relevant quantum computer exists. That is harvest-now-decrypt-later, and it is why key exchange must move first. NIST IR 8547 deprecates RSA and elliptic-curve cryptography after 2030 and disallows them after 2035. FIPS 203, 204 and 205 (ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA) were published in August 2024, and OpenSSL 3.5 (April 2025) ships all three, so migration is possible today. Quantum Guard is the tool that tells an organisation where it stands and moves it.
