    NIST Post-Quantum Standardization & IR 8547 Transition Timeline

    Key milestones governing enterprise migration from RSA/ECC to FIPS 203, 204, and 205.

    August 2024 — NIST Publishes Final PQC Standards (FIPS 203, FIPS 204, FIPS 205)

    FIPS 203 (ML-KEM) for general key encapsulation, FIPS 204 (ML-DSA) for lattice-based digital signatures, and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA) for stateless hash-based signatures.

    March 2025 — NIST Selects HQC as Backup KEM & April 2025 OpenSSL 3.5.0 Released

    Hamming Quasi-Cyclic (HQC) selected as code-based backup to ML-KEM. OpenSSL 3.5.0 ships native ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA, and default hybrid group X25519MLKEM768.

    TODAY () — Migration Window Open

    You are here. NIST IR 8547 (Transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography Standards, initial public draft November 2024) sets the 2030 / 2035 dates below; FN-DSA (FIPS 206) is in draft. Immediate priority: eliminate HNDL exposure on long-shelf-life data by deploying X25519MLKEM768 hybrid TLS 1.3 and building internal PQC PKI.

    After 2030 — Classical Public-Key Algorithms DEPRECATED (NIST IR 8547)

    RSA-2048/3072, ECDSA P-256/P-384, ECDH, X25519-only, Ed25519, DH, and DSA are officially deprecated by NIST IR 8547.

    After 2035 — Classical Public-Key Algorithms DISALLOWED (NIST IR 8547)

    Unprotected classical asymmetric algorithms are disallowed. Note: during the comment period NIST said on the pqc-forum list that the 2035 disallowance was not intended to apply to hybrid modes pairing an approved PQC algorithm (e.g., ML-KEM-768) with a classical one.
